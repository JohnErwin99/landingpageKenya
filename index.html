Before you switch

Fair questions.

If yours isn't here, call +254 20 878 8888 and ask a person.

What does KSh 20,000 a month actually cover?
It is the entry point for a small-team bundle — cloud calling with your own numbers, plus your choice of the connectivity, recording and security add-ons. Final pricing depends on user count, number of lines and which services you take. The quote you receive is fixed, itemised and has no hidden activation charges.
Do we have to give up Microsoft Teams or Webex?
No — that is the point of Direct Routing. Your team keeps working in the app they already know, and Iristel becomes the carrier behind it, so Teams or Webex starts making and taking real calls on your business numbers.
Can we keep our existing phone numbers?
Yes. Number porting is part of every migration we run. Your numbers stay live throughout the process, and we schedule the final cutover outside your business hours.
How long does a migration take?
Small teams are usually live within a couple of weeks of signing; larger multi-site setups depend on porting timelines and how many locations need survey work. You get a dated plan with the quote, not an estimate after the fact.
Who do we call when something breaks?
One number, one team, whether the issue is a dropped call, a licence, or an endpoint flagged by ESET. Because everything sits on one contract, there is no vendor to blame and no ticket to forward.
Is this only for Nairobi businesses?
No. Cloud services reach any site with a working internet connection, and we support multi-branch businesses across Kenya. Tell us your locations on the call and we will confirm what each one needs.
